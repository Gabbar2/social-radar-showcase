Privacy Policy

This is a template for a showcase build of Social Radar. Replace this text with your own policy before connecting real accounts.

Social Radar keeps no database of personal data. Connected-account tokens and drafts are stored in your own browser (and optionally your own cloud storage). Messages and comments are fetched from each platform on demand and are not kept on the server.

To remove your data: press Disconnect on an account, remove the app in the platform's settings, and clear this site's data in your browser.
